# AarogyaAI

AarogyaAI is a lightweight AI-powered healthcare platform designed to make basic health guidance more accessible, understandable, and available, especially for rural communities.
It brings multiple essential health tools together in one simple interface, including symptom checking, AI health chat, skin screening, blood report analysis, BMI calculation, voice assistance, and PDF health summaries.

💡 **Vision** : Use technology to make early health guidance easier to access, while keeping the experience simple enough for anyone to use.

## Features

- 🩹 Symptom Checker
- 🌐 Supports English, Hindi & Hinglish
- 🚨 Automatic escalation for red-flag symptoms
- 👨‍⚕️ Provides basic guidance and recommended doctor category
- 🤖 AI Health Chat
- 🔊 Text-to-speech support
- 🛡️ Safety-aware responses for sensitive/crisis-related queries
- 🧴 Skin Screening
- 🩸 Blood Report Analyzer
- ⚖️ BMI Calculator
- 🌙 Dark / Light mode


# ❤️ Why AarogyaAI?

Healthcare information can often be difficult to access, understand, or navigate, especially for people in underserved communities.
AarogyaAI explores how AI + simple web technology can provide an early layer of health guidance while encouraging users to seek professional medical care when necessary.
The goal isn't to replace doctors.
**The goal is to help people take the first informed step.** ❤️

## Disclaimer

AarogyaAI provides general AI-assisted health information and is intended for educational and early-guidance purposes only.
It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it is not an emergency medical service.

🚨 In a medical emergency, contact local emergency services or a qualified healthcare professional immediately.
