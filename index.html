Care Guidance

Basic treatment & home care guidance

General, safe self-care guidance — hydration, rest and diet pointers — with clear disclaimers. Always confirm medicines and dosages with a doctor or pharmacist.

💧

Hydration

Drink enough fluids and oral rehydration solutions when fever or vomiting is present.

😴

Rest & Recovery

Adequate sleep and reduced physical exertion help the body recover faster.

🍲

Diet Support

Light, nutritious meals and adequate protein intake can support recovery and immunity.

Sample plan — Viral Fever / Flu (general guidance only):
Day 1–2: Rest, sip fluids/ORS regularly, monitor temperature every 6 hours.
Day 2–3: Light, easily digestible meals; continue fluids; take any fever medicine only as directed by a doctor or pharmacist.
See a doctor promptly if: fever crosses 102°F / 39°C, lasts beyond 3 days, or you notice difficulty breathing, persistent vomiting, severe weakness, or a rash.
⚠️ This guidance is for educational purposes and does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
